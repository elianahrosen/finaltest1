Donors, PACs pour millions into New Jersey governor’s race

Democrat Mikie Sherill won the Nov. 4 election with 57% of the vote.

By Eliana Rosen

December 11, 2025

Republican Jack Ciattarelli (left) represented New Jersey's 16th legislative district in the state's General Assembly from 2011 to 2018. Democrat Mikie Sherill served four terms in United States Congress, representing NJ's 11th district.
Wikimedia Commons

National eyes were on New Jersey’s 2025 gubernatorial race. The election, between Republican former state representative Jack Ciattarelli and Democratic Congresswoman Mikie Sherill, was one of the most expensive races in the state’s history. On Nov. 4, Sherill won 57% of the vote, becoming the next governor-elect of the Garden state. Over the course of the election, both candidates have raised over $40 million from individuals and PAC donations.

Support for these candidates is bigger than just the state where they reside. As an off year election, one year before the midterms and three years from the next presidential election, New Jersey’s governor’s race often gets a national spotlight, said Ian Vanderwalker, senior counsel and manager of elections and government at the Brennan Center for Justice.
